# NEXORA: Color Palette (V1)

| Role | Name | HEX | RGB |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary (backgrounds) | Deep Navy | #0B1220 | 11, 18, 32 |
| Secondary (buttons, elements) | Electric Blue | #2F6BFF | 47, 107, 255 |
| Accent (key word or number) | Mint Cyan | #19D3C5 | 25, 211, 197 |
| Light | Off-White | #F4F7FB | 244, 247, 251 |
| Neutral (secondary text) | Slate | #8A97AB | 138, 151, 171 |

## Usage ratio
- 60% Deep Navy or Off-White (backgrounds)
- 30% Electric Blue (elements, buttons, shapes)
- 10% Mint Cyan (accent only: one key word or number per cover)

## Text rules
- Main text on Deep Navy: Off-White.
- Secondary text on Deep Navy: Slate.
- Accent word: Mint Cyan.
- Avoid small text in Electric Blue on Deep Navy (low contrast).
  Use it for large text, shapes and buttons.
